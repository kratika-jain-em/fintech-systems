# How I Work with Fintech Teams

I help fintech startups build and operate **reliable, production-grade payment systems**, across architecture, engineering execution, and real-world constraints.

---

## How I can help!

### 1. Payments System Stabilisation

**Focus**  
Stabilise production systems and reduce failure rates.

**What I do**
- Deep dive into production flows, logs, and failure points  
- Identify systemic issues (not surface bugs)  
- Fix high-impact reliability gaps  

**Outcome:**
Reduced failures, fewer incidents, predictable system behaviour  

---

### 2. 0->1 Fintech Architecture Setup

**Focus**  
Design a system that can scale beyond MVP.

**What I do**
- Define modules, APIs, and data flows  
- Design async patterns and scaling approach  
- Align with real-world constraints  

**Outcome:**
Build-ready architecture without early rewrites  

---

### 3. Engineering Team Reset

**Focus**  
Fix delivery issues in growing teams.

**What I do**
- Redesign hiring and evaluation  
- Build onboarding structure  
- Improve ownership and delivery model  

**Outcome:**
Faster onboarding, better delivery, clearer ownership  

---

### 4. Fractional Engineering Leadership

**Focus (2–3 days/week)**  
Provide senior engineering leadership without full-time hire.

**What I do**
- Own roadmap and execution  
- Unblock teams  
- Align engineering with product  

**Outcome:**
Consistent delivery and direction
